At CVMatch, we recognize the importance of protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and manage personal data when you access or use our CV-matching platform.

Our approach to CV and job description data

CVMatch is designed to process CVs and job descriptions for matching purposes only. The original CV file is not intended to be retained after text extraction. Extracted text, submitted job descriptions, and analysis results may be stored to provide scan history and related account features. See the sections below for the details and retention rules that apply.

7. How CV and Job Description Data Are Processed

7.1 CV Upload and Text Extraction

When you upload a CV, CVMatch processes the file to extract text for analysis. The original file is intended to be processed temporarily rather than retained as a stored CV document.

After extraction, the application may retain extracted CV text and matching results to support scan history and account functionality.

Extracted CV text is still personal data when it relates to an identifiable person. It is therefore subject to the protections described in this Policy.

7.2 Job Description Processing

Submitted job descriptions are processed to identify role requirements, skills, keywords, and other relevant terms for comparison with the CV.

Job description text may be retained alongside scan history where the feature is available and according to the applicable retention period.

7.3 Matching and Analysis

CVMatch compares relevant information from the CV and JD to produce matching results. These results may include matching scores, skill coverage, detected terms, and categories.

The analysis is intended to assist the user. It is not a definitive judgment of a person's competence or suitability for employment.

7.4 User Responsibility

Users should submit only CVs and job descriptions they are authorized to use. They should avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in submitted documents.
